    The brews

    Giving a warm cup back

    Pregnancy takes a lot of drinks off the table: alcohol, most coffee and many herbal teas. Each brew is a caffeine-free cup made for one stage.

    Before pregnancy

    Vanilla rooibos tea

    A naturally sweet, caffeine-free everyday tea.

    First trimester

    Ginger lemon tea

    Real ginger and lemon for queasy days.

    Second and third trimester

    Vanilla oat latte

    A warm or iced latte mix that carries calcium and vitamin D.

    After birth

    Cinnamon oat malt

    A warm malted grain drink with protein, for recovery.

    Breastfeeding

    Chocolate oat latte

    Protein and extra fluids, hot or iced.

    No licorice, hibiscus, raspberry leaf or other herbs that carry pregnancy cautions.

    Safety and quality

    Designed for pregnancy from the start

    Works alongside a prenatal

    • Vitamins and minerals at 10 to 25% of daily needs per bar
    • Two bars a day plus a typical prenatal stay under every upper limit
    • Folic acid capped; no preformed vitamin A
    • Iron paired with vitamin C to help absorption
    • Labels use FDA pregnancy and lactation Daily Values

    Conservative on ingredients

    • No herbs in pregnancy bars beyond culinary ginger
    • Caffeine-free, confirmed by testing
    • No sugar alcohols or artificial sweeteners
    • Allergens: almond (tree nut) and sesame; dairy-free
    • Nut-free options planned

    Tested and traceable

    • Every lot of higher-risk ingredients tested for lead, cadmium, arsenic and mercury
    • Flours heat-treated, since the bars are not baked
    • The optional herbal lactation version is sold as a supplement, labeled not for use in pregnancy, with herbs tested lot by lot
    • Formulas reviewed with a nutrition expert before launch

    Every product is in development. Each bar and brew will come in a range of familiar flavors. Nothing on this page is a finished formula or a medical claim, and your answers will shape what we build.
